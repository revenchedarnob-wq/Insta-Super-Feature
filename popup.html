⚡ Feature Guidewhat each main button does
  • Download ButtonTiny save button on the current Reel, video, or photo.
  • Browser Quick SaveZero-setup downloads through the browser itself. Keeps working even without the local helper.
  • Best Quality ModePrefers the highest loaded media source.
  • Progress BarClean scrub bar for current videos.
  • Auto UnmuteStarts videos with sound when possible.
  • Enter Love ReactPress Enter to like or unlike current media.
  • Sponsor BlockerHides obvious sponsored posts.
  • Up/Down NavigationOn Home feed, Down jumps forward and Up jumps back.
  • Focus ModeReduces side clutter for a cleaner feed.
